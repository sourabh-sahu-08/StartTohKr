# StartTohKr — SIH Pitch Deck Outline

## Slide 1: Title & Hook
- **Title:** StartTohKr
- **Tagline:** "Where Ideas Meet Opportunities."
- **Visual:** The StartTohKr logo alongside the 7 ecosystem nodes (Startup, Govt, Investor, etc.)
- **Hook:** "Innovation doesn't fail because of a lack of ideas. It fails because of a lack of connection. What if government procurement was as seamless as a social network?"

## Slide 2: The Problem
- **Siloed Ecosystems:** Startups struggle to find government pilots. Government struggles to discover verified, scalable solutions.
- **Outdated Procurement:** Traditional tender portals are clunky, slow, and lack modern matching capabilities.
- **Bias & Inefficiency:** Evaluating deep-tech proposals is hard and often prone to systemic bias.

## Slide 3: The Solution — StartTohKr
- **Unified Innovation Hub:** A single platform where the entire ecosystem converges.
- **Beyond a Tender Portal:** A dynamic marketplace that operates like a blend of LinkedIn and Product Hunt.
- **The Lifecycle:** Idea → Showcase → Discovery → Pilot → Procurement → Scaling.

## Slide 4: Key Features (Live Demo Focus)
1. **Challenge Marketplace:** Government posts problems, startups apply seamlessly.
2. **Evaluator Console (Blind Mode):** AI-assisted, bias-free technical evaluation.
3. **Pilot Workspace:** Kanban tracking and live KPI monitoring for active government pilots.
4. **StartTohKr AI Copilot:** Smart matching, proposal drafting, and ecosystem insights.

## Slide 5: Business Impact & Scalability
- **For Government:** Faster tech adoption, cheaper procurement, proven ROI through tracked pilots.
- **For Startups:** Direct access to massive B2G (Business-to-Government) markets.
- **Scalability:** Built on Next.js, PostgreSQL, and scalable cloud infrastructure. Ready to support national deployment.

## Slide 6: Future Roadmap
- **Phase 1 (Done):** Core matchmaking, profiles, and challenge marketplace.
- **Phase 2 (Done):** AI Copilot and Pilot Management Workspaces.
- **Phase 3 (Upcoming):** Integration with actual government payment gateways and digital credentialing (DigiLocker).
